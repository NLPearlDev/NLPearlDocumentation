The Realtime API gives your app a live, speech-to-speech conversation with your Pearl: you stream the end user's voice in and play Pearl's voice back as it speaks. It is the same Pearl as on your phone numbers, with the same [flow](/pages/pearl_vibe), call history, [webhooks](/pages/webhooks), recordings and [billing](/pages/billing). Only the start differs: your app starts the call through the API, with no phone number.

## Before you start

- A [voice Pearl](/pages/getting_started#2-create-your-agent-with-pearlvibe) that is **active**. The call runs its [published](/pages/getting_started#5-publish-your-agent) version.
- Its **Pearl ID**: on your Pearl's overview page, in the **Settings** popover.
- An API key, or an OAuth app with the **`pearls:websocket`** scope (**Make Realtime API calls** in the platform). See [API Authorization](/api-reference/authorization).
- One of the two audio formats, the same in both directions:

| `audioFormat` | Audio | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ulaw_8000` (default) | 8 kHz μ-law | Phone quality. The format of Twilio and most phone systems |
| `pcm_24000` | 24 kHz 16-bit PCM | HD. Websites and apps |

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the call">
    From your backend, call [Create Call](/api-reference/v2/realtime/create-call). It returns the `url` to open and a `ticket` valid for 60 seconds.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST "https://api.nlpearl.ai/v2/Pearl/<pearlId>/WebSocket" \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer <AccountId>:<SecretKey>" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{ "endUserId": "crm-77812", "audioFormat": "pcm_24000" }'
    ```

    `endUserId` is [your own id for the person on the call](/api-reference/v2/realtime/create-call#param-end-user-id), such as a customer id: send the same value for the same person every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the WebSocket">
    Open the `url` with the ticket, within 60 seconds. Pearl sends `ready`, then you stream.

    ```javascript
    const ws = new WebSocket(`${call.url}?ticket=${call.ticket}`);
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Your API key stays on your backend. A browser or an app gets only the `url` and the `ticket`, which open this one call, once.
</Warning>

## Stream audio

- **Audio is binary**, in both directions: raw bytes in the call's format, with no base64 and no JSON.
- **Send one 20 ms frame every 20 ms**, in real time, for the whole call. When the end user is quiet or muted, send silence, not nothing.
- **Pearl sends one 20 ms frame every 20 ms** too, with silence while it listens. Play the frames through a small jitter buffer of 60 to 100 ms.
- **Interruptions need no code.** When the end user talks over Pearl, it stops speaking and the next frames are already silence: there is nothing to clear on your side.

## Events

Text messages are JSON events. Pearl sends three:

| Event | When |
| --- | --- |
| `ready` | Once, right after you connect |
| `transfer` | The flow [transferred](/pages/transfer_call) the call. It carries the target and the data collected so far; carrying the call on is up to you |
| `ended` | The call is over, with the `reason`. Pearl then closes the connection |

To hang up, send `{"type":"hangup"}`. After the call, the call history and the [webhooks](/pages/webhooks) show it with `channel: "websocket"`.

## Errors and limits

| What happens | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The create is refused with `No agent is available…` | Every one of your Pearl's [agents](/pages/agents) is on a call, phone calls included | Retry later, or give your Pearl more agents |
| The connection is refused with `401` | The ticket expired, was already used, or is missing | Create a new call and connect right away |
| The call ends with `no_audio` | 60 seconds without a message from you | Keep sending silence frames |
| The call ends with `max_duration` | Your Pearl's Max Call Duration was reached | Set a longer duration on your Pearl |

A message is at most 16 KB. [Stream Call](/api-reference/v2/realtime/stream-call) has every message, end reason and limit.
