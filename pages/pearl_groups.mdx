## Overview

A **Group** brings together several Pearls - voice, text, or a mix of both - that serve the same customers or the same part of your business.

When Pearls belong to the same Group, they can share:

- **Contacts** - the same contact records across every Pearl in the Group.
- **Interaction history** - a combined view of the calls and chats a contact had with any Pearl in the Group.
- **Collected information** - the structured data gathered by any Pearl in the Group.
- **Memory** *(optional)* - a single, shared memory for each contact.

<Info>
A Pearl can belong to **one Group at a time**. Moving it into another Group removes it from the previous one.
</Info>

---

## Individual vs Shared Memory

Two related settings control how a contact is remembered.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Individual Memory" icon="brain" iconType="light">
    A per-Pearl setting: **Memory**, set to **On** or **Off** in the **Knowledge Base** tab of the Pearl editor. When on, that Pearl remembers a contact from one conversation to the next - **within that single Pearl only**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared Memory" icon="users" iconType="light">
    A **Group-level** setting. When enabled, every Pearl in the Group accesses and updates the **same memory** for a contact, so context carries over between Pearls.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
Enabling **Shared Memory** at the Group level is not enough on its own. Each Pearl must **also** have its own **Memory** set to **On** (in its Knowledge Base) to take part in the Group's Shared Memory.
</Warning>

## Create a Group

<Steps>
<Step title="Click New group">
At the top of the **Pearls** page, click **New group**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-new-group.png" alt="New group button at the top of the Pearls page" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-new-group.png" alt="New group button at the top of the Pearls page" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>

<Step title="Name the Group">
In **Create group**, enter a **Group name** (up to **50 characters**), pick its colour, then click **Create group**. The new Group opens.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-create-dialog.png" alt="Create group dialog with the name field, the colours and the Create group button" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-create-dialog.png" alt="Create group dialog with the name field, the colours and the Create group button" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

</Steps>

<Tip>
To rename a Group or change its colour later, use the **…** menu next to its name on the left (**Rename**), or click its colour dot.

## Assign Pearls to a Group

<Steps>
<Step title="Select the Pearls">
In **All Pearls**, click **Select**, then tick one or more Pearls. A bar appears at the bottom with the number selected, **Move to group**, **Delete**, and **Cancel**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-select.png" alt="Two Pearls selected in All Pearls with the Move to group bar" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-select.png" alt="Two Pearls selected in All Pearls with the Move to group bar" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>

<Step title="Move them">
Click **Move to group**, choose the destination Group, then click **Move**. A Pearl's current Group is marked **Current**. To take Pearls out of their Group, choose **No group**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-move-dialog.png" alt="Move dialog listing the available Groups" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-move-dialog.png" alt="Move dialog listing the available Groups" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also move a single Pearl from its **…** menu (**Move to group**), or add Pearls from inside a Group with **Add Pearls**: tick the Pearls to add (**No group** shows the ones not in a Group yet, **All** shows every Pearl), then click **Add Pearl**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-add-pearls.png" alt="Add Pearls dialog of a Group with one Pearl ticked" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

## Inside a Group

Click a Group on the left to open it. It has three tabs, starting on **Assigned Pearls**:

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned Pearls** | View and manage the Pearls that belong to this Group. |
| **Shared Contacts** | Browse the contacts shared across the Group's Pearls. |
| **Group Settings** | Configure the Group name, Shared Contacts, Shared Memory, Additional Details, Contact Retention, and Delete Group. |

In **Assigned Pearls**, the toolbar lets you filter by status (**Any status**) and by type (**Any type**), **sort** (Last edited, Date created, Alphabetical, newest or oldest first), **Select** Pearls (the bar then offers **Remove from group**), **Add Pearls**, and switch between the **List** and **Two columns** views. The search at the top of the page searches the Pearls.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-assigned-pearls.png" alt="Assigned Pearls tab of a Group with its toolbar and Pearls" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-assigned-pearls.png" alt="Assigned Pearls tab of a Group with its toolbar and Pearls" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

<Warning>
Removing a Pearl from a Group does **not** delete it: it stays in your Pearls, out of any Group. Only the **Delete** action permanently deletes a Pearl.
</Warning>

## Group Settings

The **Group Settings** tab is where the Group's behavior is defined. Changes are saved immediately.

<Steps>
<Step title="Group Name">
Change the name of this Group with the pencil next to it.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-settings-name.png" alt="Group Name in Group Settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-settings-name.png" alt="Group Name in Group Settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>

<Step title="Shared Contacts, Shared Memory & Additional Details">
These three switches control what Pearls in the Group share about each contact. **Shared Memory** and **Additional Details** appear once **Shared Contacts** is on.

- **Shared Contacts** - Pearls in the Group use the same contacts and interaction history. Turning it off asks for confirmation, and stops storing new contacts for the Group.
- **Shared Memory** - every Pearl accesses and updates the same memory for each contact (see the [memory warning](#individual-vs-shared-memory) above).
- **Additional Details** - additional information collected through variables is stored on the contact and shown under **Collected Info**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-settings-contacts.png" alt="Shared Contacts, Shared Memory, and Additional Details switches" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-settings-contacts.png" alt="Shared Contacts, Shared Memory, and Additional Details switches" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>

<Step title="Contact Retention">
Choose how long contact data is kept before it is automatically deleted: **Forever**, or a number of days. Click the settings icon to change it, enter the **Retention period** in days, click **Save**, then confirm.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-settings-retention.png" alt="Contact Retention in Group Settings, set to 365 days" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-settings-retention.png" alt="Contact Retention in Group Settings, set to 365 days" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

<Warning>
Retention-based deletion is permanent. Once a contact is deleted based on the configured period, it cannot be recovered.
</Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Delete Group">
Click **Delete Group**, then confirm, to permanently delete the Group. The Pearls assigned to it are **not** deleted - only the Group and its shared-contact configuration are removed.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/groups-settings-delete.png" alt="Delete Group in Group Settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/groups-settings-delete.png" alt="Delete Group in Group Settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Shared Contacts

The **Shared Contacts** tab lists every contact shared across the Group's Pearls, with a search (**Search contacts**), a refresh button, and a sortable table:

| Column | |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone Number** | The contact's phone number. |
| **First Name** / **Last Name** | The contact's name, when known. |
| **Email** | The contact's email, when known. |
| **Reference ID** | An external reference, when provided. |
| **Last Contact** | The date and time of the most recent interaction. |

Click a row to open the contact.

## Best Practices

<Tip>
- Group Pearls that serve the **same customers** or the **same workflow**.
- Enable **Shared Memory** only when cross-Pearl continuity is genuinely useful - and remember to set each Pearl's own **Memory** to **On**.
- Use **variables** for structured information and **Shared Memory** for conversational context.
- Review **Contact Retention** before handling production contact data.
- Name Groups after the team, customer journey, or business process they represent, and give each its own colour.
</Tip>
