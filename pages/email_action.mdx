The **Email** node sends an email from your Pearl: a confirmation, a quote, a recap, or follow-up information. Placed in the conversation, it sends the email as soon as the conversation reaches it. Placed after the call, it sends it once the conversation ends. It works on voice and text Pearls alike.

## How to configure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the node">
    In the [PearlVibe editor](/pages/pearl_vibe), click **Add Node** and pick **Email** in the **Actions** section:

    - of the **In-Call** tab (**In-Chat** on a text Pearl) to send it during the conversation;
    - of the **Post-Call** tab (**Post-Chat** on a text Pearl) to send it once the conversation ends.

    You can also ask PearlVibe to add one for you in the chat (**Build** mode).

    To configure it by hand, click it on the canvas and open it with the pencil of its tile in the chat (**Manual edit**), or hover it and click **Edit Manually**. Your changes are kept once you click **Save Changes**.

## How to connect your email to Pearl

By default, emails go out from Pearl's default email address. To send from your own business address, connect it once: click **Connect Email** in the **From** list of an Email node, or go to **Settings → Email Providers** and click **Connect**. The address is then available to every Email node of your workspace.

The **Email Provider** panel asks for:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Provider** | **Gmail**, **Office365**, or **Custom** for any SMTP server |
| **Sender email** | The address your emails are sent from |
| **Sender password** | The password Pearl signs in with: your regular password or an app password, depending on your provider |
| **Send a test email to** | *(Optional)* An address to receive a test email when you click **Send test** |

Click **Save** to keep the provider. Pick yours below for the details.

<Note>
Email providers are workspace settings. You need the **Manage advanced settings** permission to save, test, or delete one; without it, you can see the provider but not change it.
</Note>
