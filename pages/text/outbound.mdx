## Setting Up Outbound Campaigns

Once you have created a Pearl, you can use it to run outbound text campaigns. Outbound settings are configured inside your Pearl, under **Outbound Settings**, so your Pearl starts conversations with your leads following the conversational flow you defined.

<Info>
**What are outbound campaigns?**

Outbound campaigns let your Pearl start conversations with a list of leads through your connected messaging channels, following the conversational logic you defined for consistent and effective outreach.
</Info>

---

## Connect a Channel

Unlike a voice campaign, an outbound text campaign isn't tied to a phone number, it's connected to a **messaging channel**. First set up the channel in your workspace settings, then open your Pearl's page, click **Settings**, and pick it in **Text Channel**.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-channel.png" alt="The Text Channel list in the Settings of a text Pearl's page" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-channel.png" alt="The Text Channel list in the Settings of a text Pearl's page" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

Outbound campaigns can run on **WhatsApp**, **SMS**, and your own system through a **Generic** channel. Follow the dedicated guides to set one up:

<Warning>
**WhatsApp requires an approved template.** WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with a template approved by Meta: your Pearl's opening sentence must match one word for word before the campaign can go live. See [Outbound campaigns and templates](/pages/text/channels/whatsapp#outbound-campaigns-and-templates).

<Note>
Telegram can't start conversations: a Telegram bot can only answer users who message it first, so Telegram is available for inbound only. On an outbound Pearl, Telegram channels are greyed out in the **Text Channel** list.
</Note>

## Outbound Settings

Configure your campaign from the **Outbound Settings** tab of your Pearl, in the [PearlVibe](/pages/pearl_vibe) flow editor. Changes are saved automatically in your Pearl's draft: publish the Pearl to use them in live conversations.

<Note>
**Message Limit**, **Auto-close After Inactivity**, **Response Delay**, and **Inactivity Follow-up** are specific to text Pearls. **Budget**, **Mask Sensitive Info**, **Default Timezone**, **Calling Hours**, and **Webhooks** work the same way as on [voice campaigns](/pages/outbound).
</Note>

### Budget

Set an optional spending cap for the campaign, in dollars. Once the budget is reached, Pearl stops starting new conversations for that campaign. You can increase it at any time as long as you have enough credit on your account.

### Mask Sensitive Info

Replace personal details with `****` in everything that is kept after the conversation. The transcript is always kept: **Off** stores it as-is, **On** masks it and opens the masking settings below.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-transcript-options.png" alt="Mask Sensitive Info set to On, with its masking settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-transcript-options.png" alt="Mask Sensitive Info set to On, with its masking settings" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **PII detection** | **Auto** (default) lets the AI decide what is personal information, such as names, phone numbers, or card numbers. **Manual** masks only the variables you pick, the same way in every conversation. |
| **Variables to mask** | *Manual only.* The variables whose values are masked. Their values are replaced with `****` wherever they appear, including inside other variables. With no variable selected, nothing is masked (a warning tells you so). |
| **Mask personal details in** | Where masking applies: **Transcript**, **Summary**, **Variables**, **Action logs**, and **Leads** (all checked by default, at least one stays checked). Unchecked parts keep the original values. In Manual mode, this setting reads **Mask the variables in**. |
| **Send masked payloads** | **On**: webhooks and post-chat actions receive the masked values (`****`). **Off** (default): they receive the original values. |

<Info>
With **Auto** detection, if a customer writes *"My credit card number is 1234-5678-9012-3456"*, the transcript stores it as *"My credit card number is \*\*\*\*"*, so you can review interactions safely without exposing sensitive data.
</Info>

The maximum number of messages Pearl sends before automatically handing the conversation off to a human. You can set between **5 and 1000** messages (default **100**).

### Auto-close After Inactivity

How long a conversation can stay inactive before Pearl closes the chat and triggers the post-chat summary. You can set between **1 and 72 hours** (default **24 hours**).

### Response Delay

How long Pearl waits before replying. When a lead sends several messages in a row, Pearl answers them together instead of replying to each one.

Choose from **ASAP (no delay)** up to **5 minutes**, in 30-second steps (default **ASAP**, which means Pearl replies as soon as it is ready).

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-response-delay.png" alt="The Response Delay list, from ASAP to 5 minutes" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-response-delay.png" alt="The Response Delay list, from ASAP to 5 minutes" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

<Note>
  Each campaign has its own response delay, separate from your inbound activity.
</Note>

### Inactivity Follow-up

When **On** (default **Off**), Pearl sends follow-up messages when the lead stops replying after Pearl's last message, to re-engage the conversation.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-inactivity-followup.png" alt="Inactivity Follow-up set to On, with one follow-up after 60 minutes" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-inactivity-followup.png" alt="Inactivity Follow-up set to On, with one follow-up after 60 minutes" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

- **Up to 3 follow-ups**: each one is a **delay** plus an optional **message** (up to 1,000 characters). Click **Add follow-up** to add the next one.
- **Minimum delay**: at least **30 minutes** between follow-ups for outbound.
- **Default**: a single follow-up sent after **60 minutes**.
- **Within the auto-close time**: the delays of all follow-ups together can't exceed [Auto-close After Inactivity](#auto-close-after-inactivity) (*"Total delay … exceeds the auto-close limit"*).

<Note>
Leave a message empty and Pearl writes the follow-up itself, from the conversation so far. A message you write can use your variables (e.g. the lead's first name) to keep the re-engagement personal.
</Note>

<Warning>
On **WhatsApp**, follow-ups are sent as free text, which WhatsApp only accepts within **24 hours** of the lead's last message. They can't reach a lead who hasn't replied to the opening template yet.
</Warning>

### Calling Hours

Define the hours during which Pearl is allowed to send outbound messages. By default, messages go out **Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00**. Calling hours respect each lead's timezone, so everyone is contacted at an appropriate local time.

### Webhooks

Send conversation and lead events to an external endpoint, useful for tracking performance and keeping your CRM in sync. Turn **Webhooks** **On** (default **Off**), then fill in the **Calls** and **Leads** lines (labelled as on voice Pearls):

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-webhook.png" alt="Webhooks set to On, with the Calls and Leads lines" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-webhook.png" alt="Webhooks set to On, with the Calls and Leads lines" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

- **Calls**: called at key points in the conversation lifecycle (when the chat starts and when it ends), with details like the outcome and any data collected during the conversation.
- **Leads**: called whenever a lead changes status (at the start and end of the conversation), with the lead's full information and current status. Use it to update your CRM in real time.

Each line has:

- **URL**: your endpoint.
- **Version** (the `V1`/`V2` pill): the payload format, default **V2**.
- **Signature** (the key icon): optionally sign the requests with one of your tokens (or create a **New token**) so your endpoint can verify they come from NLPearl. **No signature** sends them unsigned.

<Note>
If your endpoint is behind a firewall, allow the NLPearl IP addresses listed in [IP Whitelisting](/pages/ip_whitelist).
</Note>

<Tip>
Use Webhooks only if external tools need event notifications. For actual automations, rely on **Post-Chat Actions** tied to how the conversation ended.
</Tip>

## Project-Level Settings

A few settings live at the **Project** level rather than in the Outbound Settings tab. Open them with the **Settings** button on your Pearl's page: next to **Text Channel**, you'll find **Agents**, **Chat Retention**, and **Lead Retention**.

### Agents

Sets how many **simultaneous agents** run this campaign, i.e. how many conversations can be handled at the same time, capped by your account's agent quota. A higher number allows faster outreach. Click the **Agents** value and pick a number: it is saved right away.

<Card
  title="Agents"
  icon="users"
  iconType="light"
  href="/pages/agents"
>
  Learn how to manage the agents assigned to your inbound and outbound activities.
</Card>

---

### Retention

Click the **Chat Retention** value to open the retention window. Set the **Retention period** in days (leave it empty to keep conversations **Forever**), then choose what is removed when they expire: delete all conversation data (on by default), or turn it off to pick the **Transcript**, **Summary**, or collected **Variables**. Click **Save** to apply.

<div className="block dark:hidden"><Frame><img src="/images/light_mode/text-outbound-chat-retention.png" alt="The retention window opened from Chat Retention" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>
<div className="hidden dark:block"><Frame><img src="/images/dark_mode/text-outbound-chat-retention.png" alt="The retention window opened from Chat Retention" className="rounded-[14px]" /></Frame></div>

<Note>
The window is shared with voice Pearls: it's titled *Call retention* and also lists **Recording**, which doesn't apply to conversations.
</Note>

---

### Lead Retention

Outbound only. Click the **Lead Retention** value to open the **Lead retention** window, and set the **Retention period** in days before leads are automatically deleted (leave it empty to keep them **Forever**). This removes the entire lead. Click **Save** to apply.

## Managing Leads

Open the **Leads** tab on your Pearl's page to add, view, and manage the people Pearl will contact. Leads are shown in a searchable, paginated table with default columns **Created**, **Phone Number**, **Time Zone**, **Reference ID**, and **Status**, plus one column per variable used in your Pearl. Filter the table with the **Status** and **Columns** chips next to the search field. On a text Pearl, statuses use chat wording, such as **In Chat**, **Chat Successful**, and **Chat Unsuccessful**.

### Refreshing and Deleting

Select one or more leads (or select all across the current filter). A bar appears at the bottom with the number selected, then:

- **Reset status**: resets the leads to **New** so they re-enter the queue — handy to re-contact already-processed leads (Completed, Chat Unsuccessful, etc.).
- **Delete**: permanently removes the selected leads, after a confirmation.
- **Cancel**: clears the selection.

### Outside Calling Hours

When a lead's local time falls outside your campaign's [calling hours](#calling-hours), a **moon icon** appears on its Time Zone column — the lead is "sleeping" and won't be contacted until its window opens. Hover the icon to see **Outside calling hours**, with the lead's local time and UTC offset.

Click a lead to open its details panel. Use the arrows or **↑/↓** keys to move between leads. The header shows the lead's name (or phone number), its **Lead ID** (copyable), and its status.

**Status** — change the lead's status from the editable dropdown; the change is applied instantly. This simply updates the lead's status label (e.g. **Chat Successful**, **Chat Unsuccessful**, **New**) — for example, set it back to **New** to re-contact the lead.
